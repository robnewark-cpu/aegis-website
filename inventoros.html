What InventorOS includes

Projects

Create innovation projects and ingest the evidence that later analysis and packages depend on.

Human vs AI analysis

Compare human versus AI contribution signals across architectural, coding, and logic decisions.

Auditable timeline

An event stream with date, actor, source, action, and evidence — built for later reconstruction, not for silent edits.

Evidence packages

Generate and export packages (PDF, Word, JSON) for qualified counsel to review. Software does not decide inventorship.

IP intake pack

A structured intake path and review queue so a human still approves what leaves the tenant.

Same platform identity, RBAC, tenant isolation, and audit log as other AegisOS modules. Confirm access in a demo.
