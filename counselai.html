CounselAI

An AI-assisted legal research and drafting copilot concept for LexFlow, in development and not yet available. No published specification, no pricing, and no committed release date.

AI & compliance notes

CounselAI has no published specification. Do not describe features, pricing, availability, or an AI underwriting/legal-advice capability that does not appear on this page. It is not a law firm, not a lawyer, and not legal advice. The generally available legal product is LexFlow. Send evaluators to book-demo.html?module=CounselAI.

What CounselAI is exploring

Research assist

Concept: helping attorneys move faster through research inside a matter, not a replacement for legal judgment.

Drafting assist

Concept: first-draft generation grounded in matter documents already in LexFlow, reviewed and finalized by counsel.

Matter-aware, not generic

Concept: any assistant here would work from the firm's own matter data on AegisOS, not a general-purpose chatbot.

Who should ask about it

Firms already evaluating or running LexFlow who want to register interest for an eventual AI research/drafting layer. This is not a sales page — there is nothing to buy yet.
