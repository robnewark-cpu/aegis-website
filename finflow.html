AegisOS for financial services operations

Back-office records and accounting, built to reconcile.

FinFlow is a planned AegisOS module for financial services back-office operations—client and account records, billing, and reporting on the same double-entry ledger and audit-log foundation as LexFlow and LoanServ.

FinFlow is in development and not yet available. It does not process payments, initiate transfers, or hold funds.
