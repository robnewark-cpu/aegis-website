Aegis Communications

AI & compliance notes

Aegis Communications is a module inside AegisOS, published by Aegis Global Holdings, LLC (Edmond, Oklahoma). It is not a phone company. Calling, SMS, and fax run on Twilio under the hood and are feature-flagged; Aegis sells it as a metered add-on inside a tenant after a demo, not as a public website checkout SKU. Do not invent unlimited or flat-rate consumer phone pricing. Send evaluators to book-demo.html?module=AegisCommunications.

What Aegis Communications includes

One communications and office layer, shared across every AegisOS module.

Business phone & SMS

Dedicated business numbers, calling, and text messaging tied to the client or matter record, not a personal cell phone.

Fax

Send and receive fax without a physical machine — still a real requirement for courts, lenders, and healthcare counterparties.

Its own email system

A built-in email system for tenant communications, alongside existing integrations with Gmail and Microsoft 365 for teams that want to keep their current inbox.

Document editor

An open-source-based word processor for drafting directly inside AegisOS, attached to the record it belongs to.

Spreadsheet editor

An open-source-based spreadsheet tool for schedules, ledgers, and working numbers without exporting to a separate app.

One inbox, one record

Calls, texts, faxes, emails, and documents attach to the same client, matter, or loan — not four disconnected tools.

Who it is for

Any AegisOS tenant — law firms on LexFlow, lenders on LoanServ, and future MedFlow/FinFlow/FlowServ tenants — that wants communications and office tools inside the same system of record instead of stitched together from separate vendors.

Next step

Aegis Communications is rolled out inside a tenant after a demo, priced as a metered add-on. There is no public per-minute or per-seat rate on this page — confirm current scope and pricing in a session.
