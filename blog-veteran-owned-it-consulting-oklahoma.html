Veteran-owned IT consulting from Edmond, Oklahoma

Aegis Global Holdings, LLC is a veteran-owned technology company based in Edmond, Oklahoma. The firm does two things: enterprise IT consulting, and AegisOS, a practice-management platform with a generally available legal product (LexFlow) and a lending product in design-partner early access (LoanServ).

Why location and ownership still matter

Enterprise buyers, government contractors, and local organizations still search for “veteran-owned IT consulting Oklahoma” and “Edmond technology firm.” Those queries only resolve correctly if the company name, city, and VOSB status appear in visible copy, structured data, and third-party listings. Aegis publishes that package on the About page and the homepage FAQ.

What we actually deliver

  • Application management — keep production systems monitored, patched, and owned.
  • Data integration — connect legacy and modern systems without a one-off spreadsheet in the middle.
  • Web design and custom platforms — internal and client-facing applications built to enterprise security expectations.
  • Security and compliance advisory — practical control design. AegisOS is not FedRAMP authorized and MedFlow is not HIPAA certified; we do not advertise certifications we have not earned.
  • AegisOS — operations software for legal and lending teams. See AegisOS and LexFlow.

How to work with Aegis

Start with a proposal request, a product demo, or the on-site chat assistant. Email info@aegisglobalholdings.com. We typically respond within 24 business hours. For organizations that want to shape LoanServ before general availability, see the Design Partner Program.

Aegis is not Aegis Roofing, Aegis Bio-Systems, or other Edmond businesses that share the word “Aegis.” The legal entity is Aegis Global Holdings, LLC, website aegisglobalholdings.com.
