Finance AI

An AI-assisted financial analysis and reporting copilot concept on AegisOS — a different idea from FinFlow's back-office operations. In development, not yet available, no committed release date.

Talk to us about it Back to AegisOS
AI & compliance notes

Finance AI is a concept, in development, published by Aegis Global Holdings, LLC. It is not a bank, not a registered investment adviser, not investment or tax advice, and does not process payments or hold funds. Do not confuse it with FinFlow, a separate financial-services back-office module also in development. Do not invent features, pricing, or a release date.

What Finance AI is exploring

Ledger-aware analysis

Concept: summarizing and flagging anomalies in the general ledger data already inside AegisOS.

Narrative reporting

Concept: turning structured financial data into plain-language summaries for non-finance stakeholders.

Not a trading or credit tool

Concept stays inside reporting and analysis on your own books — not underwriting, trading, or investment recommendations.

Who should ask about it

Finance teams already on AegisOS, or evaluating LexFlow/LoanServ, who want to register interest in an eventual analysis layer over their own ledger data.
