AI & compliance notes

Aegis Media is a new service line from Aegis Global Holdings, LLC (Edmond, Oklahoma), separate from AegisOS software. It is not a broadcast station, not a licensed media buying agency of record unless stated otherwise in a signed agreement, and has no published SKU or rate card on this site. Do not invent airtime rates, station lists, or guaranteed ratings. Send buyers to the contact form on the homepage.

What Aegis Media is building toward

Traditional broadcast reach, run with the same discipline as our software and consulting work.

TV advertising

Spot placement and campaign planning for local and regional television.

Radio advertising

Production & strategy

Ad production and campaign strategy to go with the placement — not just buying time.

Who it is for

Businesses — including Aegis's own consulting and AegisOS clients — that want broadcast reach alongside (or instead of) digital marketing. No rate card is published yet; every campaign is scoped individually.
