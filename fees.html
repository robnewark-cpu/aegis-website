What you cannot buy here

LoanServ, AegisShield, and Enterprise seats

LoanServ is generally available for records, billing, ledger, and audit log — but ACH is not live, so it stays a demo. AegisShield is mapping software, not a FedRAMP ATO (use the $3,000 kickoff for advisory work). Multi-office Enterprise seats are custom.

Aegis Communications (phone, fax, email & office suite)

The module exists in AegisOS: phone, SMS, fax, its own email system, and an open-source docs/sheets office suite. See the Aegis Communications page. Twilio is feature-flagged. Aegis does not operate a phone company. We sell it as a metered add-on inside a tenant after a demo, not as a public SKU.

Anything not listed

MedFlow, FinFlow, FlowServ, CounselAI, FinanceAI, and HealthAI are not shipped. Application management, data integration, custom apps, and larger FedRAMP programs are proposals. Paying $1,500 for AegisPay does not buy ACH, an ATO, or a law firm.
