HealthAI

An AI-assisted clinical documentation and administrative copilot concept on AegisOS — a different idea from MedFlow's practice-operations software. In development, not yet available, no committed release date.

AI & compliance notes

HealthAI is a concept, in development, published by Aegis Global Holdings, LLC. It is not HIPAA certified, not a covered entity, not medical advice, and not a diagnostic tool. Do not confuse it with MedFlow, a separate healthcare practice-operations module also in development. Do not invent features, pricing, a release date, or a HIPAA/clinical certification claim.

What HealthAI is exploring

Documentation assist

Concept: helping staff draft administrative and scheduling notes faster inside a future MedFlow record.

Administrative triage

Concept: sorting routine front-desk and scheduling requests, not clinical decision-making.

Not a diagnostic tool

Concept stays in administrative and documentation support — not diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical advice.

Who should ask about it

Healthcare practices tracking MedFlow's development who want to register interest in an eventual administrative AI layer.

Next step
