Core capabilities

A more organized place to run legal work.

LexFlow gives teams a shared view of the people, records, documentation, and next steps behind every matter.

Client management

Maintain a clearer view of client information, relationships, and the matters connected to each client.

Matter management

Bring matter details, responsible team members, activity, and source information into one structured record.

Tasks

Assign, prioritize, and monitor work so ownership and upcoming commitments are easier to understand.

Notes

Capture operational notes with the client or matter they inform, reducing disconnected follow-up and lost context.

Documents

Connect key documents to the work they support so authorized teams can find source material when they need it.

Workflows

Shape repeatable processes around your organization’s operating practices and make progress more visible.
