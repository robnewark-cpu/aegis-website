Financial infrastructure

Trust accounting and billing your firm can rely on.

This is the accounting core that separates LexFlow from a records tool: money movement is reconciled, checked, and logged, not just tracked.

Trust / IOLTA accounting

Three-way reconciliation keeps bank balance, book balance, and per-matter client ledger balance in agreement, with discrepancies surfaced instead of buried.

Billing & invoicing engine

Time entry, rates, and invoice generation are built on a concurrency-tested engine, so simultaneous billing activity across a firm doesn't produce lost time or duplicate charges.

Automated conflict checking

New clients, parties, and matters are checked automatically against existing records across the firm, so conflicts surface before they become a problem.

Immutable audit log

Every financial and record action is written to a hash-chained audit log, so the history of who did what, and when, can't be quietly altered after the fact.

Double-entry general ledger

A full chart of accounts, journal entries, trial balance, and financial statements—firm accounting that stands on its own, not a spreadsheet workaround.
