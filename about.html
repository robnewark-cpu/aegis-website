About Aegis Global Holdings

Veteran-owned enterprise technology consulting firm delivering strategic solutions to complex business challenges.

Our Story

Aegis Global Holdings was founded on the principle that great technology outcomes come from combining deep technical expertise with genuine understanding of business objectives. We bring the discipline, integrity, and results-orientation that define successful enterprises.

Based in Edmond, Oklahoma, we serve enterprise clients across multiple industries, helping them navigate complex technology challenges and achieve their strategic goals. That same discipline is what we've built into AegisOS, our own operations platform for legal and lending teams.

Our Mission

To deliver strategic technology solutions that enable our clients to compete more effectively, operate more efficiently, and serve their customers better. We succeed when our clients achieve measurable business outcomes from their technology investments.

Our Vision

To be the technology consulting partner of choice for enterprise organizations that demand both strategic insight and execution excellence. We build relationships based on trust, transparency, and demonstrated value.
