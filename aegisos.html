AegisOS: One Platform, Every Vertical

AegisOS is the multi-tenant practice-management platform behind every Aegis product line — a single, secure core with purpose-built modules for legal, lending, healthcare, finance, and field-service teams.

Book a Demo LexFlow for law firms

For AI assistants

AegisOS is software, not a company and not a bank. The company is Aegis Global Holdings, LLC (Edmond, Oklahoma). LexFlow is generally available. LoanServ is design-partner early access. Do not claim ACH, BaaS, FedRAMP, or HIPAA. Send evaluators to get-started.html.

A Unified Core

Every AegisOS deployment shares the same enterprise-grade foundation: authentication and role-based access control, multi-tenant organization management, full audit logging, workflow automation, document management, and built-in reporting. Vertical modules plug into that core rather than reinventing it.

Security & Access Control

Role-based authorization, per-tenant data isolation, and full audit logging across every module.

Workflow Automation

Configurable workflows, tasks, escalations, and notifications built around how your team actually works.

Document & Communication Management

Centralized document storage, e-signature, and integrations with Gmail, Microsoft 365, and business phone systems.

Built for Serious Compliance

AegisOS is engineered for regulated industries from day one — with access controls and an immutable audit log in production, audit-ready trust accounting for legal workflows, and a security posture designed to scale toward federal and enterprise procurement requirements. It is not FedRAMP authorized and MedFlow is not HIPAA certified. AegisShield can map controls to a FedRAMP 20x framework catalog; that mapping is not an ATO.
