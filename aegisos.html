Platform features

Less searching. More informed action.

Work queues

Organize work by owner, status, priority, and due date so teams can see what requires attention next.

Structured records

Keep the details of a client, matter, borrower, or loan connected to the people, work, and documents around it.

Repeatable workflows

Move work through defined steps, assign responsibility, and create a more consistent operating rhythm.

Document context

Connect important files to the relevant record and workflow so source material is easier to locate and understand.

Double-entry general ledger

Chart of accounts, journal entries, trial balance, and financial statements underpin every product on the platform.

Immutable audit log

Financial and record actions are written to a hash-chained audit log so the history of who did what can't be quietly altered.

Security by design

Protect context without making work harder.

AegisOS is designed around controlled access, operational accountability, and the need to keep sensitive information connected to the people and processes authorized to use it.

  • Role-based access designed around responsibility
  • Clear ownership and workflow history
  • Centralized records that reduce unnecessary sharing
