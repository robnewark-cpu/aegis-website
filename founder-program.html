Founder Program FAQs

Who is the Founding Customer Program for?

It is for legal and lending organizations that want to improve operational visibility and are interested in giving practical feedback as AegisOS evolves.

What does lifetime founder pricing mean?

Your selected founder rate remains available while your organization continues as an active customer under the program terms. We will review the applicable plan and terms before enrollment.

How do we know whether LexFlow or LoanServ fits?

Book a demo and tell us how your team currently manages records, documents, work queues, and workflows. We will discuss the product path that fits your operation.
