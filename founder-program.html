Design Partner Program FAQs

Who is the Design Partner Program for?

It is for legal and lending organizations that want to improve operational visibility and are interested in giving practical feedback as AegisOS evolves—including organizations that want early access to LoanServ.

What does negotiated design partner pricing mean?

Your rate and terms are set individually based on scope, timeline, and product fit, and confirmed with you before enrollment. LexFlow customers outside the program use our standard published tiers.

How do we know whether LexFlow or LoanServ fits?

Book a demo and tell us how your team currently manages records, documents, work queues, and workflows. We will discuss the product path that fits your operation.
