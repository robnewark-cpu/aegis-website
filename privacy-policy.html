1. Information We Collect

This section describes the categories of information AegisOS collects and how it is technically stored, based on a direct review of the platform's source code.

1.1 Account and Usage Information

When you use AegisOS, we collect account information you provide (such as name, email address, and organization details), along with the matters, records, and financial data you enter into the platform as part of using the Service.

1.2 Log and Audit Information

AegisOS maintains an audit log of actions taken within the platform (such as record creation, edits, and financial postings) for security, accountability, and compliance purposes. This log is a core part of how the Service operates and cannot be disabled.

1.3 Browser Storage

AegisOS uses your browser's built-in sessionStorage to keep you securely signed in while you use the Service. Browser storage may contain:

  • Your authentication (access) token
  • Your refresh token
  • Limited user profile information necessary to display your account within the application (such as your name and role)

This information is used solely for authentication, access control, and platform functionality — it allows AegisOS to recognize that you are signed in and to enforce the correct permissions for your account. It is automatically cleared when you sign out or close your browser tab, and it is not shared with any third party.

AegisOS does not use browser storage of any kind for advertising, behavioral tracking, or marketing purposes.

1.4 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

AegisOS does not use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Tracking cookies
  • Cross-site tracking technologies
  • Marketing pixels
  • Third-party analytics platforms

The only technologies AegisOS uses in connection with your browser are strictly necessary authentication and security technologies, described in Section 1.3 above, required for you to sign in and use the Service securely.

If this changes in the future — for example, if we introduce analytics to help us understand product usage — we will update this Privacy Policy before any such technology is deployed, and we will provide any consent mechanism required by applicable law at that time.

1.5 Website Chat Widget

Our public marketing website (aegisglobalholdings.com) uses a chat widget to answer visitor questions and route inquiries to the right person at Aegis. If you use this chat widget, the messages you send are processed by our website chat provider in order to respond to you and, where you request it, to connect you with our team. This is separate from the AegisOS application itself and from the browser storage described in Section 1.3 — it applies only to conversations you choose to start on our public website.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to: provide, operate, and maintain the Service; authenticate users and enforce access controls; maintain the audit trail required for the platform's compliance and security features; respond to support requests; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your data to serve advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We do not share your information with third parties except: (a) with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as infrastructure and email delivery providers) under confidentiality obligations; (b) when required by law or legal process; or (c) with your consent.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and platform data for as long as your organization maintains an active subscription, plus any additional period required by our audit-logging and record-retention obligations or applicable law. Browser session storage described in Section 1.3 is retained only for the duration of your active session.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 8.

6. Security

AegisOS uses industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), multi-factor authentication support, and tenant-isolated data storage. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes — including introducing any new tracking or analytics technology — we will update the effective date above and provide notice as required by applicable law.

8. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to Aegis Global Holdings at info@aegisglobalholdings.com.
