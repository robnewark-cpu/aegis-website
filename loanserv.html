Core capabilities

Bring lending operations into a shared workspace.

LoanServ helps connect the business records and work queues that lending teams need to coordinate effectively.

Borrowers

Maintain borrower details and relationships in a record that connects to relevant loan activity and documentation.

Loans

Organize loan records, status, responsible team members, and the operational activity associated with each loan.

Collections

Support a clearer collections process by connecting outreach, tasks, notes, and documents to the underlying record.

Documents

Keep supporting documents linked to the borrower, loan, or work item where authorized teams expect to find them.

Workflows

Define repeatable operational paths, make ownership visible, and keep work moving through the appropriate next step.

Operational visibility

Give teams a practical view of work in progress, upcoming actions, and the details that guide better follow-through.

Built for follow-through

Make the next action easier to identify, assign, and complete.

Loan operations require context across people, records, documentation, and activity. LoanServ keeps those pieces closer together, helping teams reduce the friction of searching across disconnected tools.

  • Connect borrower details and loan records
  • Track work with defined owners and next steps
  • Keep collection activity and documents in context
